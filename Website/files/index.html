Catalog QA

Product Catalog Audit

  • ProblemA 12-item electronics and home-office catalog had no visibility into which listings were actually sale-ready.
  • ActionTracked SKU, category, brand, price, and status per item, then built a live status breakdown.
  • Result50% Completed, 33% flagged Needs Review, 17% caught as Failed and routed for fixes before going live.

Image-to-SKU Mapping Audit

  • ProblemProduct photos weren't reliably matching their listed SKU across a 12-item bag catalog.
  • ActionCross-checked each SKU's source image against its mapped image, one by one.
  • ResultCaught 3 mismatches (Laptop Backpack, Mini Daypack, Gym Duffel Bag) and corrected all of them — 12/12 passed QA.
